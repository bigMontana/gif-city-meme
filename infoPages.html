Frequently Asked Questions

How do I make a video?

Click on a template you like. There will be slots to upload faces. Sometimes you can upload more than one angle if the video allows it. But that's okay if you don't, just one image will work, but it looks much better with all the face angles filled up. Then your faces get added to the video, and you can scrub through the video to preview. Then you choose your render settings and click render. Download it when it's finished and that's it!

How do I get more credits?

Purchase more credits on the homepage or on your profile page or any page basically...

Where can I download my past renders?

In your profile page there will be links to your 3 most recent renders. Click on one to download.

Something went wrong with my render, and it charged me credits

Contact me directly on X @GergPerson, my name is Greg and we will work it out.

How long do credits last?

Credits never expire. You can use them anytime you want.

How do I save faces so I don't have to upload them every time?

In your profile page you can spend credits to unlock slots for your most-used faces. That way you don't have to constantly upload the same faces

Can I upload my own video and use it as a template?

Currently, no. But that is a possibility in the future.
